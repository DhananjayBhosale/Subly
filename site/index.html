How it works

Three easy steps.

From video to finished captions, without leaving your Mac.

  1. 1

    Add Video

    Drop in an MP4, MOV or audio file. Your projects wait for you on the home screen.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Check the spoken language — Subly detects it when the Whisper model is installed. Pick your caption tracks and add names and brands so they’re spelled right.

  3. 3

    Edit & Share

    Click a caption on the video to fix it, pick a look, then export your video or caption file.

Features

Small app. Big captions.

Private

Everything runs on your Mac. Your videos are never uploaded.

In sync

Every track shares the same timings. If captions feel early or late, Sync nudges them all by 0.05 s.

Edit on the video

Click a caption on the video to fix a word, then click away to save. Drag it to move the captions.

Learns your spelling

Fix “yah” to “ye” once and Subly writes “ye” in every new caption. See or forget what it learned in Settings.

Caption looks

Clean, Bold Pop, Karaoke, Boxed, Minimal, Typewriter and Gradient Pop, with your own font, size, colour and gradient.

Made for Hinglish

Hindi in Devanagari, the same words in English letters, or both. The optional Apex model is tuned for Hindi to Hinglish.

Share anywhere

Export a video with captions burned in for Reels, Shorts and Instagram, or save SRT, VTT, TXT or JSON.

Names spelled right

Type the names and brands in your video, like “iPhone” or “Fitbit”, and Subly listens for them.

Undo anything

Every edit, split, merge and sync nudge can be undone. Even Listen Again, which redoes the whole video.

Want more languages? Download the optional Whisper model (about 99 languages) inside the app. Models download only when you ask.

Install

Install in four steps.

  1. Download Subly.dmg, open it and drag Subly to Applications.
  2. Open Subly. macOS says it can’t check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down.
  4. Click Open Anyway next to Subly. You only do this once.

Why the extra step?

Subly isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS can’t check it the first time you open it. It’s a one-time step, and all of Subly’s code is public on GitHub for anyone to read.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Subly is free and open source under the MIT licence. Download it, use it, share it.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Subly listens, writes and translates on your Mac, so your videos never leave it. The optional speech models download inside the app, and only when you ask.

Which languages does it support?

Subly uses Apple’s built-in speech recognition out of the box, and detects the spoken language automatically once the free Whisper model is installed. For more, download the optional Whisper model (about 99 languages) or Apex, which is tuned for Hindi to Hinglish. Translations use Apple’s on-device translation.

Why does macOS show a security warning?

Subly isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS can’t check it the first time you open it. Click Done, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple Silicon running macOS 26 or later.
